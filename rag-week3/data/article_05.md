# Notification Settings and Troubleshooting

## Overview

CloudDesk can send notifications for important events such as ticket updates, assignments, mentions, and system events.

Users can configure their notification preferences according to the options available in their organization.

## Notification Types

CloudDesk notifications may include:

- Ticket assignment notifications.
- Ticket update notifications.
- Comment or mention notifications.
- Status change notifications.
- System notifications.
- Email notifications.

The exact notification options may depend on the organization's configuration.

## Managing Notification Preferences

To manage notification preferences:

1. Sign in to CloudDesk.
2. Open your user profile.
3. Select "Notification Settings".
4. Review the available notification categories.
5. Enable or disable the required notification options.
6. Save the changes.

Changes to notification settings apply to future notifications.

## Email Notifications

If email notifications are enabled, CloudDesk sends notification messages to the email address associated with the user account.

If notifications are not received, verify that:

- The correct email address is registered.
- Email notifications are enabled.
- The messages are not being filtered as spam.
- Your organization's email system allows CloudDesk messages.

## Browser Notifications

Browser notifications may require browser permission.

If browser notifications are disabled:

1. Open browser notification settings.
2. Find the CloudDesk site.
3. Allow notifications.
4. Return to CloudDesk.
5. Verify that browser notifications are enabled in Notification Settings.

## Notification Troubleshooting

| Problem | Possible Cause | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| Email notification not received | Email notification disabled | Enable email notifications in Notification Settings |
| Notification goes to spam | Email filtering | Check spam or junk folders |
| Browser notification not displayed | Browser permission disabled | Allow CloudDesk notifications in browser settings |
| Only some notifications appear | Notification category disabled | Enable the required notification category |
| Notification sent to wrong email | Incorrect account email | Verify the email address associated with the account |
| Notifications stopped suddenly | Organization settings changed | Contact the CloudDesk administrator |

## Troubleshooting Missing Email Notifications

If email notifications are not arriving:

1. Open Notification Settings.
2. Verify that email notifications are enabled.
3. Verify the email address associated with your CloudDesk account.
4. Check your spam or junk folder.
5. Search your inbox for CloudDesk notification messages.
6. Contact your email administrator if CloudDesk messages are being blocked.

## Troubleshooting Browser Notifications

If browser notifications are not displayed:

1. Verify that browser notifications are enabled for CloudDesk.
2. Check the browser's site permissions.
3. Make sure the CloudDesk tab or application is not blocked from sending notifications.
4. Verify notification settings inside CloudDesk.
5. Refresh the CloudDesk application.

## Organization-Level Notification Controls

Some notification settings may be controlled by the CloudDesk organization administrator.

If a notification option cannot be changed by an individual user, contact the organization administrator.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why am I not receiving email notifications?

Check whether email notifications are enabled, verify the registered email address, and check your spam folder.

### Why are browser notifications not appearing?

Browser notification permission may be disabled. Check the browser's site permissions and allow CloudDesk notifications.

### Can users change all notification settings?

Not necessarily. Some notification settings may be controlled by the organization administrator.

### What should I do if only one type of notification is missing?

Check whether the corresponding notification category is enabled.

## Notification Security

Do not click notification links that appear suspicious or request sensitive credentials.

If you receive an unexpected security-related notification, verify the event directly in CloudDesk.
