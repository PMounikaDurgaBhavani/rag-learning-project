## Reading the traces (written 2026-11-04 after the runs)

The detectors above ran over 64 requests (40 fast-mode, 12 before-fix, 12 model-planner). The modes below are ordered by harm, not by count, the way Week 8 ranked them; each is read from the trace ids in the table.

**1. `wrong_policy_version_in_prompt` — 10 of 64 (all in the before-fix run), severity: wrong answer about money, integration-created.** Trace `614be377162f496f` (T03): three of five passages were HC-009; `bbdd1c8a2da54e4c` (C-12002, the customer who read about a 14-day window): five of five. The decision was still right because the refund check is computed, but the sources the agent would cite were the retired policy. Root cause: similarity ranking has no notion of "retired"; the version is an account fact. Regression tests: four checks in `tests/test_week12.py` named in `week12/closed_loop.json`. After the fix: 0 of 52 requests.

**2. `wrong_decision_or_outcome` — 5 of 64, severity: wrong answer or unnecessary escalation.** Three are the same ticket, T09 (`382905cdb3f749a8`, `78f911db5de24870`, `d7eb34edaeea44a7`): an implicit refund that the rule-based classifier reads as a billing question, so the account is never checked and the case escalates as "not found". One is C-12011 (`50f43af3449f41e1`), a correct replacement decision escalated by a guardrail false positive (see mode 3). One is R05 under the model planner (`495b35dd7d5d4b51`): right tools, right order, but the model's own search query returned no HC-008 passage and the version rule escalated rather than cite HC-009 — *the safe failure the fix created*. Regression tests: the DOA false positive has two; the T09 classifier gap is open and listed under weaknesses.

**3. `decision_contradicted_by_draft` — 3 of 64, severity: blocks a deflection, integration-created (it only exists because the guardrail exists).** `be7ca2a27c944f78` (T18): the first draft failed `no_pii_leak` because the model copied a support email address out of the article; the second draft passed and was sent — the rule is conservative on purpose (no email addresses in replies at all). `50f43af3449f41e1` (C-12011): the false positive above; the second draft still mentioned the 14-day DOA window, so the ticket escalated with the correct decision attached for the human. `323b51d6b94d4655` (T03, model planner): the draft was cut off and tripped the window assertion on the fragment.

**4. `reply_cut_off_or_scaffolding` — 20 of 64, severity: embarrasses the client.** Two different causes share the detector. In the fast-mode run, 9 of 14 cut-offs were the 300 s generation deadline firing after 4–12 tokens (e.g. `13bd0514cefe4493`, T10, four tokens in 363 s) — the laptop was swapping under other applications and the 1.5B model fell below one token per second. The other 5 hit the 160-token limit because the model opens with "Customer Inquiry / Dear Customer" boilerplate (`fcc93099aee54209`, T03). The first cause is hardware; the second is the drafter and is the same M3 mode Week 5 named.

**5. `escalation_tag_wrong` — 18 of 64, severity: pages Tier 2 for nothing or misses a Priority SLA, measured on the raw draft.** The 1.5B model drops `[ESCALATED-PRIORITY]` on Priority tickets (`d1472e0fc32d45e3`, T02) exactly as Week 6 found. Since 13:50 the tag is normalised in code after the assertions run and recorded as `post_processing`, so no sent reply was wrong; the mode is kept in the table because the raw-draft measurement is the honest one.

**6. `missing_amount_or_ticket_id` — 8 of 64, severity: annoys the user.** Every instance is a cut-off reply that ended before the amount (`6ac708c8353c4916`, T07) or, once, before the ticket id. Not an independent mode: it disappears with mode 4.

**7. `guard_forced_tool_call` and `wall_clock_or_budget` — 2 of 64 each (the same two traces, `d1c0f93c315747ba` and `c656fd42a80d4414`), severity: cost and latency only, integration-created.** The model chose the first two tools correctly, the third lap could not start before the loop's 300 s budget, and the guard ran the help-centre search. Right answers, path finished by code. On a quiet machine these laps take 15–60 s.

**Not a failure mode:** the 7 escalations the cases expected (legal, PII probe, data deletion, out of scope, missing order, conflicting evidence, Enterprise pricing not in the corpus) all escalated, 5 of them without a model call.

**What changed since Week 5.** Mode 1 and mode 7 did not exist before the integration; mode 2's R05 variant did not either. Weeks 5–6's M1 (refusing an answerable question) is now rare because the lexical/rerank bypass and the MCP search keep the right article; M3 (cut off) is worse on this hardware than it was in Week 6.
