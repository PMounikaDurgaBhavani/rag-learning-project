# Refunds, Support Tiers and Escalation

## Overview

This article describes when a CloudDesk subscription charge can be refunded, how refunds are returned, and how tickets from Priority support customers are escalated.

## Refund Eligibility Window

A subscription charge can be refunded in full only when the refund is requested within 30 days of the charge date.

A charge made more than 30 days before the refund request is not eligible for a refund. Support agents must not promise or process a refund for a charge outside the 30-day window.

When a charge is outside the refund window, the customer can turn off automatic renewal from Organization Settings > Billing so that the next charge does not occur.

## Duplicate Charges

If the same invoice was charged more than once, every duplicate charge is refunded in full, regardless of the 30-day window.

## Refund Amount and Timing

A refund is always for the exact amount that was charged. The reply to the customer must state that amount.

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.

CloudDesk does not offer discounts, credits or partial refunds in place of a refund.

## How to Request a Refund

To request a refund:

1. Open Organization Settings.
2. Select "Billing".
3. Select "Invoices".
4. Choose the invoice for the charge.
5. Select "Request Refund".
6. Enter the reason for the request and submit it.

Only users with billing permissions can request a refund.

## Support Tiers

CloudDesk offers two support tiers: Standard and Priority.

Standard tickets receive a first response within 1 business day.

Priority tickets receive a first response within 1 hour.

## Escalating Priority Tickets

Every ticket from a Priority tier customer is escalated to a Tier 2 agent.

A reply to a Priority ticket must include the tag [ESCALATED-PRIORITY].

## Ticket References

Every reply to a customer must quote the ticket ID, for example CD-10423, so the customer can refer to it later.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I get a refund after 30 days?

No. A charge older than 30 days is not eligible for a refund. Duplicate charges are the only exception.

### How long does a refund take?

A refund reaches the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.

### Who can request a refund?

Only users with billing permissions can request a refund.

### Can I get a discount or credit instead of a refund?

No. CloudDesk does not offer discounts, credits or partial refunds in place of a refund.
