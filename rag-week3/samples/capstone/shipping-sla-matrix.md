# Hardware Shipping SLA Matrix

## Overview

CloudDesk ships hardware (desk phones, headsets and badge readers) to customers whose plan includes hardware. This article lists the delivery service levels, who is entitled to expedited shipping, and what happens when a shipment is late, lost or arrives damaged.

## Delivery Service Levels

| Region | Standard delivery | Expedited delivery | Carrier |
|---|---|---|---|
| United States and Canada | 5 to 7 business days | 2 business days | NorthPost |
| United Kingdom and EU | 6 to 8 business days | 3 business days | EuroParcel |
| Australia and New Zealand | 8 to 10 business days | 4 business days | PacificLine |
| Rest of world | 10 to 15 business days | not available | PacificLine |

Business days exclude weekends and public holidays in the destination country. The service level starts on the day the order status changes to "shipped", not on the order date.

## Shipping Entitlement by Plan

| Plan | Standard delivery | Expedited delivery | Replacement shipping |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | included | paid upgrade | included for dead-on-arrival units |
| Priority | included | included at no charge | included, expedited |
| Partner (legacy terms) | included | not available | per partner agreement |

An agent must confirm the customer's plan in the entitlement record before offering expedited or replacement shipping.

## Late Shipments

A shipment is late when it has not been delivered by the last day of its service level. For a late shipment the agent can:

1. Check the tracking status with the carrier.
2. If the carrier shows no movement for 3 business days, open a carrier trace.
3. If the shipment is still undelivered 5 business days after the service level ended, treat it as lost.

## Lost or Damaged Shipments

A lost shipment or a unit damaged in transit is replaced at no charge. Replacement requests must be raised within 30 days of the shipped date. Replacement shipping follows the entitlement table above.

## Dead-on-Arrival Hardware

A unit that does not power on or fails activation within 14 days of delivery is dead-on-arrival. Dead-on-arrival units are replaced at no charge on every plan. The customer returns the faulty unit with the prepaid label included in the replacement box.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I get expedited shipping?

Priority plan customers receive expedited delivery at no charge. Standard plan customers can purchase it as an upgrade before the order ships. It is not available on partner-billed plans or to the rest-of-world region.

### My order says shipped but has not arrived. What now?

Compare the shipped date with the service level for your region. If the service level has passed, contact support with the order ID; the agent will open a carrier trace.
