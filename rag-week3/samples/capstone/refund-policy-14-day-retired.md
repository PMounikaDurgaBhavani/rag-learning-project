# Refund Policy (14-Day Window) — RETIRED

> **This policy was retired on 2026-09-14.** It applies only to accounts whose contract terms still reference the 14-day refund window (partner-billed and legacy annual agreements that have not yet renewed). All other accounts are covered by the current 30-day policy, HC-008.

## Overview

This article describes when a CloudDesk subscription charge can be refunded under the legacy terms, how refunds are returned, and what a support agent may promise.

## Refund Eligibility Window

A subscription charge can be refunded in full only when the refund is requested within 14 days of the charge date.

A charge made more than 14 days before the refund request is not eligible for a refund under these terms. Support agents must not promise or process a refund for a charge outside the 14-day window.

When a charge is outside the refund window, the customer can turn off automatic renewal from Organization Settings > Billing so that the next charge does not occur.

## Duplicate Charges

If the same invoice was charged more than once, every duplicate charge is refunded in full, regardless of the 14-day window.

## Refund Amount and Timing

A refund is always for the exact amount that was charged. The reply to the customer must state that amount.

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 7 to 14 business days.

CloudDesk does not offer discounts, credits or partial refunds in place of a refund.

## How to Request a Refund

To request a refund:

1. Open Organization Settings.
2. Select "Billing".
3. Select "Invoices".
4. Choose the invoice for the charge.
5. Select "Request Refund".
6. Enter the reason for the request and submit it.

Only users with billing permissions can request a refund.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I get a refund after 14 days?

No. Under these legacy terms a charge older than 14 days is not eligible for a refund. Duplicate charges are the only exception.

### How long does a refund take?

A refund reaches the original payment method within 7 to 14 business days.

### Which accounts are still on these terms?

Only accounts whose signed agreement references the 14-day window: partner-billed subscriptions and legacy annual contracts until their next renewal. Support agents must confirm the account's policy version in the entitlement record before quoting this article.
