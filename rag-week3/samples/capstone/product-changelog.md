# CloudDesk Product Changelog

## 2026-10-28 — Ticket Management

- Saved filters can now be shared with a team.
- Bulk status changes support up to 200 tickets per action.

## 2026-10-02 — Notifications

- Digest emails can be scheduled per time zone.
- Fixed: mobile push notifications were delayed for organizations with more than 500 agents.

## 2026-09-15 — Billing policy change

- The refund eligibility window was extended from 14 days to 30 days. The current policy is HC-008 "Refunds, Support Tiers and Escalation". The previous 14-day policy (HC-009) is retired and applies only to partner-billed and legacy annual agreements until they renew.
- Refund timing improved: approved refunds now reach the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.

## 2026-08-20 — Hardware

- Expedited delivery added for the United Kingdom and EU region (3 business days).
- Dead-on-arrival window clarified as 14 days from delivery.

## 2026-08-12 — Storage

- Maximum upload size is now configured per organization by an administrator.

## 2026-07-30 — Authentication

- Password reset links expire after 30 minutes.
- Accounts lock after repeated failed sign-in attempts; see HC-002.
